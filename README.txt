Precious Metals Premium Calculator - phone app (installable web app)

You type: asking price, weight, purity. Tap the metal at the bottom. It never reads your screen.

HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE
1. Host this folder on any HTTPS site (free: GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify). Upload every file in it.
2. On the Android phone open the site in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
3. It opens full screen with its own icon and works offline (prices need internet to refresh).

TO MAKE A PLAY STORE PACKAGE LATER
Use PWABuilder (pwabuilder.com) with your hosted URL, or Capacitor with Android Studio. Google Play needs a developer account; check Google's current fee and rules.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL OR WEBSITE] in privacy.html and [YOUR NAME] in LICENSE.
- Privacy policy must be hosted at a public URL for Play Store.
