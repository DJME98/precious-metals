Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What stays on your phone

Everything you type (prices, weights, purities, exchange rates) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to me or anyone else. Clearing the app's data erases it.

What the app asks the internet for

The app makes two kinds of requests: spot prices from api.gold-api.com and exchange rates from open.er-api.com. These requests contain no information you typed and no account or personal identifiers. As with any web request, those services can see your IP address and basic technical details, under their own policies.

What I collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app does not read your screen or other apps.

Not financial advice

The app is for information only. Prices may be delayed or wrong. Check before you buy or sell.
